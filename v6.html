SunClean ACMC – V6

End-to-end cleaning service with trained manpower, advanced machines, and performance-driven execution for maximum energy yield.

SunClean ACMC – V6 is a comprehensive annual cleaning service solution designed to ensure consistent performance of solar power plants. It combines skilled on-site manpower, advanced SunClean machines, and structured maintenance planning to deliver reliable and measurable results.

Tailored to site-specific conditions, ACMC ensures that solar panels are maintained at optimal cleanliness levels, directly contributing to higher energy generation, reduced downtime, and improved asset longevity.
