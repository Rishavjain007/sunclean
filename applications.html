Our Applications

Utility-Scale Solar Plants

SunClean robots are ideal for large-scale solar farms (50MW+), providing efficient, automated cleaning for thousands of panels across extensive areas. Our ground-mounted robots can navigate complex arrays and clean up to 1,000 panels per day.

Key Benefits:

  • Massive labor cost reduction
  • Consistent cleaning quality
  • Minimal plant downtime
  • Scalable for any farm size

Commercial Rooftops

For shopping malls, office buildings, warehouses, and other commercial structures with rooftop solar installations. Our rooftop robots provide safe, automated cleaning without risking human safety on elevated structures.

Key Benefits:

  • Eliminates rooftop safety risks
  • Operates during off-hours
  • Minimal structural impact
  • Preserves roof warranty

Industrial Installations

Manufacturing facilities, factories, and industrial parks with solar installations often face challenging environmental conditions. SunClean robots are built to handle industrial pollutants and maintain optimal panel efficiency.

Key Benefits:

  • Handles industrial contaminants
  • Reduces maintenance downtime
  • Withstands harsh environments
  • Improves energy reliability

Large Solar Farms

Agricultural solar projects, government solar initiatives, and renewable energy parks benefit from SunClean's scalable solutions. Our robots can be deployed across hundreds of acres with centralized monitoring and control.

Key Benefits:

  • Centralized fleet management
  • Remote monitoring capability
  • Customizable cleaning schedules
  • Integration with farm management systems

Floating Solar Plants

Specialized SunClean solutions for floating solar installations on water bodies. Our waterproof robots with enhanced buoyancy and corrosion resistance ensure reliable operation in aquatic environments.

Key Benefits:

  • Waterproof and corrosion-resistant
  • Stable operation on floating platforms
  • Minimal water consumption
  • Prevents algae and biofilm growth

Agricultural Solar Projects

Agrivoltaic installations combining agriculture with solar power generation. SunClean robots are designed to navigate around agricultural equipment and clean panels without disturbing crops or soil.

Key Benefits:

  • Compact design for agricultural settings
  • Minimal ground disturbance
  • Compatible with crop cycles
  • Enhances dual land use efficiency
