SunClean Hybrid – V3

Self-operating, solar-powered cleaning robot designed for scheduled, consistent, and maintenance-free solar panel cleaning.

SunClean Hybrid – V3 is a fully autonomous solar panel cleaning robot engineered for reliable, scheduled cleaning without manual intervention. It leverages time-based programming, onboard solar charging, and intelligent sensors to deliver consistent cleaning performance.

Designed for operational independence, V3 eliminates the need for apps or manual control, making it ideal for solar plants seeking low-maintenance, high-efficiency cleaning solutions.
