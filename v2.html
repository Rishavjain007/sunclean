How SunClean Portable V2 Operates

Intuitive process for uniform cleaning and improved energy yield

1

Place Robot at Start Position

Operator places the robot at the start of the solar panel row.

2

Push-Button Start

Push-button system initiates the cleaning operation.

3

Guided Cleaning Motion

Robot moves along the panel using guided motion and wet cleaning.

4

Automatic Edge Detection

Integrated sensors detect edges and stop automatically for safety.

5

Reposition to Next Table

Machine is manually repositioned to the next solar table.

👉 Faster cleaning cycles with improved safety and reduced manual effort.
