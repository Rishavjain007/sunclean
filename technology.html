Our Technology

Advanced automation and cleaning mechanisms for maximum solar efficiency

Automation Technology

Intelligent Robotic Navigation

SunClean's proprietary automation technology enables our robots to navigate complex solar arrays with precision and efficiency. Using a combination of GPS, sensor arrays, and AI-powered algorithms, our robots can autonomously clean entire solar farms without human intervention.

Automation Features:

  • AI-powered path optimization algorithms
  • Real-time obstacle detection and avoidance
  • Weather-adaptive operation scheduling
  • Remote monitoring and control capabilities
  • Self-diagnostic and reporting systems
  • Integration with plant management software

Cleaning Mechanism

Advanced Cleaning Technology

Our cleaning mechanism is specifically designed to remove dust, sand, bird droppings, and other contaminants without damaging solar panels. The system uses optimized brush materials, precise water application, and controlled pressure to ensure thorough cleaning.

Cleaning Features:

  • Microfiber brushes for gentle yet effective cleaning
  • Low-pressure water system for water conservation
  • Adjustable cleaning intensity based on soiling level
  • Contaminant-specific cleaning modes
  • Self-cleaning brush mechanism
  • Water recycling capability option
