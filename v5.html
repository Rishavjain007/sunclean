SunClean Rail-Bot – V5

Track-mounted, IoT-enabled robotic system designed for seamless multi-table cleaning with centralized control and maximum efficiency.

SunClean Rail-Bot – V5 is a fully automated, rail-mounted solar cleaning system engineered for large-scale solar plants with linear table layouts. It combines precision rail mobility, IoT-based control, and intelligent cleaning logic to deliver consistent, high-efficiency cleaning across multiple tables using a single robotic unit.

Designed for utility-scale operations, V5 significantly reduces manpower dependency while ensuring uniform cleaning, optimized resource utilization, and scalable deployment.
