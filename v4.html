SunClean Infinity – V4

Fully automated, IoT-enabled robotic system engineered for large-scale solar plants with real-time monitoring and zero manual dependency.

With remote monitoring capabilities and self-charging functionality, V4 ensures uninterrupted performance while significantly reducing operational costs and manpower dependency.
