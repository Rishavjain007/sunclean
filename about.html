Who we are

Understanding the challenges of modern solar plants

SunClean was founded with a clear understanding of the evolving needs of the solar industry. We focus on bridging performance gaps with intelligent technologies.

Dust accumulation reducing energy generation
Vegetation growth causing shading & accessibility issues
Difficulty in monitoring large-scale solar sites
Delayed identification of module & equipment faults
Rising operational and maintenance costs
Security concerns across remote installations
Lack of real-time operational visibility

At SunClean, we understand these challenges because they are the same challenges our customers face every day. Our goal is to help solar operators overcome these obstacles through intelligent and practical solutions.
