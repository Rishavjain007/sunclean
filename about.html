About SunClean

Leading the solar cleaning revolution with advanced robotic technology

Our Company

SunClean was founded with a clear mission: to solve the critical problem of solar panel soiling that reduces energy output and affects return on investment for solar power plants across India and globally.

As pioneers in solar maintenance automation, we combine expertise in solar technology, robotics, and industrial manufacturing to deliver cutting-edge cleaning solutions that maximize solar plant performance.

Our team of engineers and solar experts has decades of combined experience in the renewable energy sector, ensuring that every SunClean product is designed for reliability, efficiency, and long-term value.

Manufacturing Expertise

State-of-the-Art Manufacturing

Our manufacturing facility utilizes advanced production techniques and quality control processes to ensure every SunClean robot meets the highest standards of durability and performance.

We specialize in:

  • Precision robotic assembly
  • Industrial-grade component selection
  • Rigorous testing under simulated solar farm conditions
  • Custom engineering for specific project requirements
  • Continuous improvement through R&D

Our vertically integrated manufacturing approach allows us to maintain strict quality control while optimizing costs for our customers.

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

Our Mission

Industry Credibility

Trusted Solar Industry Partner

SunClean has established itself as a reliable partner for solar power plant operators, EPC companies, and renewable energy developers across India and international markets.

Our solutions are deployed in diverse environments including:

  • Commercial and industrial rooftop installations
  • Agricultural solar projects
  • Floating solar plants
